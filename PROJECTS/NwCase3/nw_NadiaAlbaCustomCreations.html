The Process

  1. Submit your request
  2. You will receive an email inquiring further information and include a quote of timeframes based on information provided.
  3. Upon approval, you will receive updates regularly, no less than bi-weekly for updates regarding your order.
  4. You will receive a high priority email that includes the design and what that may look like on the product you requested.
  5. Upon approval, the design is considered final and will not be further altered.
  6. Laser engraving begins, taking 2 business days to complete work.
  7. Packaging is completed, and an update for drop off/pick-up scheduling will be provided via email.
  8. Upon receipt of order, you will receive a card to complete a review of our business.
